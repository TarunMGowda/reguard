\# Reguard Dataset



This directory contains the regulatory and contract documents used by Reguard.



\## Regulatory Documents



The initial regulatory corpus focuses on Indian data protection requirements.



\### Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023



Source:

Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)



\### Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025



Source:

Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)



\## Contract Documents



The contract corpus consists of:



1\. Publicly available Indian legal contract clauses/datasets.

2\. Synthetic corporate contracts and policies created for project evaluation.



Synthetic documents are intentionally designed to contain varying levels of relevance to regulatory requirements.



\## Important Note



Reguard is an educational compliance-assistance system.



Its output identifies documents and clauses that may require further review. It does not provide legal advice or determine definitive legal compliance.
